I've found the coursework complements my professional work by deepening my understanding of modern neural architectures, simulation, and statistics.

        Alongside my industry work, I'm the founder of True Tone, a personal research project that's evolved into a full-scale product at the intersection of music and machine learning. The system applies state-of-the-art deep learning and signal processing techniques to align recorded audio with uploaded sheet music, providing real-time intonation feedback for musicians.

Music has been a passion of mine since I was little, and I regularly perform in professional chamber groups and regional orchestras. I've found the intersection of math and music to be larger than most would think, and I've given several presentations to a range of audiences to explore that intersection.
